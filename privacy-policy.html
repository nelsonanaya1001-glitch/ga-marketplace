Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Overview

GA Marketplace (“GA Marketplace,” “we,” “us”) is a Miami, Florida-based wholesale sourcing company. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit a Request for Quote (RFQ) or contact us, we collect the information you provide:

  • Your name, company, business email address, and phone number
  • The product or brand you are requesting, your estimated quantity, ship-to ZIP code, and target price
  • Any additional details you include in your message
  • Information you share when you call or email us

Our website hosting provider may also automatically record standard technical data, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, to keep the site secure and running.

How we use information

  • To respond to your RFQ and prepare quotes
  • To source products and arrange fulfillment and shipping for confirmed orders
  • To communicate with you about quotes, orders, and support
  • To operate and secure this website
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only when needed to provide our services — for example, with suppliers, carriers, and fulfillment partners involved in your quote or order, with service providers that help us run our business (such as website hosting and email), or when required by law.

Third-party services

This website is hosted by Vercel and loads typefaces from Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when you visit. Other websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices.

Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to handle your request, maintain business records, and comply with legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request its deletion, subject to our legal and record-keeping obligations. You can also ask us to stop sending non-essential emails.

Business use only

This website is intended for business customers and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was most recently revised.
